# Adverse action and reason codes

## Purpose of reason codes
Reason codes identify the main factors that increased predicted risk for an application.
They support adverse action notices and customer explanations. They do not by themselves
change the automated outcome.

## Utilisation-related codes
When revolving utilisation is high relative to peers, the scorecard assigns adverse
contribution to that feature. Policy expects clear wording that high utilisation was
material to the decision support narrative.

## Income-related codes
Low-income bins can contribute adverse reason codes. Explanations must remain factual
and must not imply prohibited bases.

## Human override
Only authorized underwriters may override an automated outcome. Overrides require
identity, timestamp, and free-text justification stored in the decision audit log.
